Game Premise
	You wake up stranded on a tiny wooden platform attached to the side of a cliff thousands 	of meters above the ground with nothing but wind, height, and gravity trying to kill 		you. You’ll scavenge, craft, and fight the environment with the objective to stay alive 	and build yourself a suspended home capable of surviving the elements.

	"The cliff doesn’t hate you — it simply doesn’t care if you fall..." No open fields or 		safe valleys, just the endless drop below and the endless climb above.


Controls
	WASD 			-> Moves your character
	Space 			-> Jump
	1-8 			-> Select hotbar
	Scroll wheel 		-> Cycle hotbar
	E			-> Interact
	Escape 			-> Open menu/pause game/close crafting menu
	TAB			-> Open/close bag & crafting menu
	Shift			-> Sprint while moving
	T			-> Show your objectives

	When holding a building hammer:
	Right mouse button      -> Opens build menu
	Left mouse button 	-> Place building

	When holding a harpoon:
	Hold left mouse button 	  -> Charge throw
	Release left mouse button -> Throw

	When holding food or water:
	Hold right mouse button -> Drink/eat

	When holding an axe:
	Hold left mouse button -> Destroy natural resources and player-made objects
